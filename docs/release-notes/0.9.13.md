> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is the first published build of Couch Session.**
> It is in daily use on the machine it was written on, but that is one PC, one television and two controllers. **Expect bugs.** Right now a bug report is worth more than a feature request.

## 🛋️ What it does

Your desk PC is also your living room console. Getting between the two means switching the display, moving the sound, fixing the resolution, turning on HDR and opening Big Picture, then undoing every one of them when you are done.

Couch Session does the whole round trip. One press out, one press back, everything as you left it.

- **Display** — switches to your TV, alone or alongside your monitors, at the resolution and refresh rate you chose. Puts your desktop and its windows back afterwards.
- **Sound** — moves audio to the TV and back, and can mute a game you left running behind you.
- **HDR** — for a whole session, or only while a game from your list is running. Works on its own at the desk, with no session involved.
- **Controller** — start and end sessions from the pad, use it as a mouse for launchers that ignore controllers, and decide what happens if it disconnects.
- **Performance** — power plan, game priority and silencing notifications for the session.

Every setting says what it does and whether it is put back when the session ends. Nothing is hidden behind a wiki.

## 🐛 Reporting a bug

> [!TIP]
> Use the **Report a bug** button in the app's footer. It writes one file to your desktop with the log, your settings and your display and audio details, then opens the issue page for you to attach it. That file is almost always where the answer is hiding.

Otherwise, [open an issue](https://github.com/Sub-Wolfer/couch-session/issues) and attach `%AppData%\CouchSession\couchsession.log`.

Especially useful to hear about:

- **Controllers other than Xbox pads and the PS5 DualSense.** Everything else should work, but only those two have been tested. A DualShock 4, a Switch Pro, an 8BitDo: a report either way is genuinely useful.
- **Games that behave oddly around the session prompt**, particularly exclusive-fullscreen ones.
- **Anything the app tells you that turns out not to be true.** Wrong text is treated here as a real bug.

## ⚠️ Known issues

> [!WARNING]
> **A game that ignores the request to close is ended outright after 8 seconds, and unsaved progress goes with it.**
> Closing asks the game politely first, the same way <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>F4</kbd> does, so most titles save and exit cleanly. A game paused on its own dialog, mid-cutscene, or simply slow does not get longer. The confirmation dialog does not currently mention this.

- **Exclusive-fullscreen games minimize when the session prompt appears.** Windows does that itself the moment such a game loses focus, and it cannot be avoided while also taking the controller away from the game. Setting a game to *borderless* fullscreen avoids it entirely.
- **HDR can quietly do nothing on a display that supports it.** The app only switches HDR when Windows reports the display as capable, and Windows does not always agree with the display itself. Nothing on screen says so, there is no way to overrule it in this build, and the log is the only place it is recorded.
- **Some windows do not go back where they were.** OBS is a reliable example. A window moved to a second monitor shortly before a session may also come back on the wrong one. Under investigation; the log now records both cases.
- **The display can be slow to sleep while a controller is connected.** A pad that streams continuously, as a DualSense does even sitting still, holds Windows' idle timer open. The app lets go after about 90 seconds of a genuinely untouched controller.
- **Couch audio set to the same device you already use does nothing.** There is nothing to move and nothing to restore. The app says so once per run and points at the setting.
- **A TV's audio output only appears in the list while the TV is on.** One seen before stays selectable, marked as off, so it can be chosen with the set cold.

## 💻 Getting it

> [!NOTE]
> **Windows 10 or 11. No installer and no runtime to install.**
> Download `CouchSession.exe` below and run it. Settings live in `%AppData%\CouchSession` and nothing is written anywhere else.

**Steam** is needed for the Big Picture half of a session. The HDR and display features work without it.
